Recording on your watch

Recording begins when you press START. AIRLOG uses GPS location, activity time, distance and speed, accelerometer and gyroscope readings where supported, ambient pressure, and diagnostic information such as battery level, available memory and capture quality. These data support session recording and experimental sensor analysis. Garmin's native FIT activity recorder may also include additional device-generated activity information, depending on your watch and settings.

Recorded sessions are saved as FIT activities on your watch. This beta has no AIRLOG server upload, advertising or third-party analytics integration. It does not automatically send sessions to an AIRLOG phone application or to Strava.

Garmin services

Your watch may synchronize the saved activity with Garmin Connect through Garmin Connect Mobile or Garmin Express, according to your Garmin settings. Garmin processes that synchronization under its own Garmin Connect Privacy Policy. This is separate from AIRLOG's local processing.

Data you submit to AIRLOG are submitted to the AIRLOG application, not to Garmin on AIRLOG's behalf. Garmin has no responsibility or liability for data submitted to AIRLOG.

Local field-test tool

Importing a FIT file is optional and requires you to select the file. The local field-test tool processes it in your browser. It stores the original imported file in browser storage for replay; analysis results can be exported when you choose. This build does not upload imported captures to an AIRLOG backend.

The separate application prototype uses illustrative sessions. Optional kite size and feeling notes are saved locally in your browser and are not connected to watch activities or imported FIT files.

Your choices and retention

You can stop recording using START. Data already saved on the watch or in Garmin Connect remain until removed through the relevant Garmin controls. Uninstalling AIRLOG does not necessarily remove saved Garmin activities.

Local browser history remains until you clear the field-test site's browser storage or your browser removes it. Clearing site data removes local imported captures and prototype notes. Files you exported or downloaded remain wherever you saved them and must be removed separately. The AIRLOG developer does not automatically receive a copy of those local records.

Contact and changes

For questions about AIRLOG's data handling, use Contact Developer on its Connect IQ listing. Do not send precise location, health information or raw activity files unless you choose to share them for a specific support request.

This notice describes this research beta. It will be updated before changes to how AIRLOG collects, uses, stores or shares user data. Jump heights and jump counts are not independently validated in this version.

This policy website

This website presents the AIRLOG privacy notice. It has no activity upload, contact form, advertising, analytics scripts or browser storage. Its hosting provider may process technical request information needed to deliver the page. AIRLOG activity recording and local analysis are described above.
